About Us

At UNLOADIN, we believe logistics should be simple, transparent, and accessible for everyone. Founded with the vision of empowering micro and small enterprises in India, we bring together advanced AI-powered technology and human-centered design to transform the way businesses move goods.

What We Do

  • We aggregate drivers and vehicles into one seamless platform, ensuring customers always get the best market rate.
  • Our system intelligently matches demand with supply, optimizing routes, reducing costs, and saving valuable time.
  • By combining local insights with cutting-edge AI, we make logistics friendly and reliable—even for semi-tech-literate business owners.

Why Choose Us

  • Trust-driven operations: Transparent pricing and dependable service.
  • Smart technology: AI agents that automate marketing, customer engagement, and operational workflows.
  • Local connection: Campaigns and communication tailored in regional languages to resonate with communities.
  • Scalable solutions: From small businesses to growing enterprises, our platform adapts to your needs.

Our Mission

To humanize logistics by blending technology with empathy—helping businesses save money, build trust, and grow sustainably.
