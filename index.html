Goods Transport by Road - Packaging Instructions

  1. Cover goods completely and secure ends with tape.
  2. Fragile items: bubble wrap corners/edges well.
  3. Use stretch wrap for items that might shift.
  4. Boxes: fill voids; cushion bottom for fragile items.
  5. Seal using strong shipping tape; avoid string.

Sending fragile items is at Consignor’s risk; UNLOADIN is not liable for damage due to poor packaging by Consignor.

UNLOADIN Data Deletion Policy

UNLD Tech Labs Private Limited ("UNLOADIN") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Data Deletion Policy outlines how you can request deletion of your personal information from our systems.

Your Right to Data Deletion

As a user of UNLOADIN services, you have the right to request deletion of your personal data that we have collected and stored. This includes data collected through our mobile application, website, and any other platforms we operate.

What Data Can Be Deleted

Upon request, we can delete the following types of personal data:

  • Account information (name, email, phone number)
  • Profile details and preferences
  • Booking and transaction history
  • Communication records
  • Location data
  • Device information
  • Any other personal data collected during your use of our services

Data Retention Requirements

Please note that certain data may need to be retained for legal, regulatory, or business purposes, including:

  • Financial records required for tax compliance and auditing (typically 7 years as per Indian law)
  • Transaction records required for dispute resolution and fraud prevention
  • Data required for pending legal proceedings or investigations
  • Information necessary to comply with Consumer Protection regulations
  • Records needed for outstanding payments or claims

Even after deletion, some anonymized or aggregated data may be retained for analytical purposes, which cannot be used to identify you personally.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your personal data, please follow these steps:

  1. Email Request: Send an email to admin@unloadin.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  2. Provide Details: Include your registered phone number, email address, and any other details that can help us identify your account
  3. Verification: For security purposes, we may ask you to verify your identity before processing the deletion request
  4. Confirmation: Once verified, we will process your request within 30 days and send you a confirmation email

What Happens After Deletion

Once your data deletion request is processed:

  • Your account will be permanently deactivated
  • You will no longer be able to access UNLOADIN services using the deleted account
  • All personal data will be removed from our active systems
  • Backup copies will be deleted during our regular backup cleanup cycles (typically within 90 days)
  • Data retained for legal purposes will be securely isolated and not used for any operational purposes

Alternative: Account Deactivation

If you wish to temporarily stop using our services without permanently deleting your data, you can request account deactivation instead. This allows you to reactivate your account in the future. Contact us at admin@unloadin.com for this option.

Third-Party Data

If your data has been shared with third-party service providers (payment processors, logistics partners, etc.) as part of service delivery, we will request them to delete your data as well. However, their deletion timelines may be subject to their own policies and legal requirements.

Children's Data

UNLOADIN services are not intended for users under 18 years of age. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 18, we will take immediate steps to delete such information.
