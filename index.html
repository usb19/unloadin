Goods Transport by Road - Packaging Instructions

  1. Cover goods completely and secure ends with tape.
  2. Fragile items: bubble wrap corners/edges well.
  3. Use stretch wrap for items that might shift.
  4. Boxes: fill voids; cushion bottom for fragile items.
  5. Seal using strong shipping tape; avoid string.
